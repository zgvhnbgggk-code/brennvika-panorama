01 · Arrival

Getting here

The cabin will be cleaned, heated and prepared before you arrive. Please check your booking confirmation for your confirmed check-in time.

Property address

Brennvika Panorama
Brennvikveien 37
8294 Hamarøy
Norway

Use the full address in Google Maps or Apple Maps.

Last part of the drive

  1. Leave the main road and turn onto Brennvikveien.
  2. Drive carefully — the road is also used by residents, children, pedestrians and local traffic.
  3. Turn right at the junction beside the electrical transformer station.
  4. Continue downhill towards the sea. Brennvika Panorama will be on the right-hand side.

When you arrive

  • Park beside the cabin without blocking the road or access for other vehicles.
  • Please remove outdoor shoes when entering the cabin.
  • Take a quick look around and contact us promptly if anything is damaged, missing or not working as expected.
  • During winter, check weather and road conditions before travelling. Suitable winter tyres are essential.

Unlock from outside

  1. Enter your six-digit door code.
  2. Press the star button *.
  3. Wait for the lock to open.
  4. Then use the handle.

You do not need to touch or activate the keypad before entering the code.

Lock the door

  1. Pull the front door firmly closed.
  2. Press the star button *.
  3. Check that the door is securely locked before leaving.

From inside, use the lock button on the indoor unit.

If the lock does not respond

  • Check that the door is fully closed.
  • Enter the code again slowly, followed by *.
  • Wait a few seconds before trying again.
  • Do not force the handle or lock.
  • If it still fails, contact us.
