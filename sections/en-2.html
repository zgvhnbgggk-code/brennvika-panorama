07 · Inside the cabin

Kitchen, laundry & waste

A few practical systems are worth knowing, especially if they are unfamiliar to you.

Induction hob & stove guard

Norwegian kitchens normally use a safety device called a komfyrvakt. It monitors the cooking area and may warn or cut power if it detects unusually high heat.

If it starts beeping while you cook

  1. Stay by the hob and reduce the heat.
  2. Check that nothing is burning, overheating or boiling dry.
  3. If everything is under control, press the button on the stove-guard sensor once to acknowledge the warning.

If power to the hob has been switched off

  1. Turn all hob controls completely off.
  2. Check that there is no unsafe overheating.
  3. Let hot cookware cool slightly if needed.
  4. Press the stove-guard sensor button once to reset.
  5. Switch the hob back on.

If the alarm continues or power does not return, stop using the hob and contact us. Do not remove, cover or disconnect the sensor.

Dishwasher · water supply

The dishwasher has a separate water supply controlled from the kitchen tap. Please leave it open.

If the dishwasher cannot fill with water, check the dishwasher water supply at the tap, open it if needed and restart the dishwasher. Contact us if the problem remains.

Washing machine

The washing machine is compact. Do not overfill it, and use only a small amount of detergent. Too much laundry or detergent can cause excessive foam.

Waste

Ordinary household waste goes in the large yellow waste bags used locally. A bag is provided under the kitchen sink. Tie it securely and take it to the roadside waste cage when full or at departure. Use the first cage by the junction towards Vassbotnen.

Do not leave full waste bags outside the cabin; they can attract birds and animals.

Something missing or not working?

Let us know early so we can help. Please do not attempt electrical, plumbing or technical repairs yourself.

Fire & emergencies

One smoke alarm is connected directly to a professional alarm monitoring service, as required by the insurance company.

If the fire alarm sounds or you discover a fire

  1. If you see or suspect fire, leave the cabin immediately and make sure everyone is accounted for.
  2. Call 110 from a safe location and give the address: Brennvikveien 37, 8294 Hamarøy.
  3. Once everyone is safe, please also call us promptly. This is particularly important if the alarm appears to have been triggered by cooking, steam or another non-fire cause.

Glimma

A scenic walking area near Presteid and the Hamsun Centre, combining coastal scenery, cultural history, tidal water and mountain views. A good choice for a less demanding outing.

Art in Tranøy

Tranøy has a long-standing artistic community with galleries, workshops and outdoor sculptures. Cafés, galleries and exhibitions can have seasonal opening hours.

Fishing

Vestfjorden offers excellent sea fishing. Sea fishing with handheld tackle is generally free, but national minimum sizes, protected species and local restrictions apply. Keep at least 100 metres from fish farms. Freshwater fishing normally requires permission or a local fishing licence.

Sea conditions change quickly. Wear a life jacket when boating and check the marine forecast.

Beaches & coastal walks

Outer Hamarøy has attractive beaches, coves and rocky coastline. The water stays cold even in summer; waves, currents and slippery rocks can create dangerous conditions. Supervise children closely near the sea.

Northern lights

From autumn through early spring, Brennvika can offer excellent northern-light conditions. Choose a clear, dark evening, reduce nearby lights, dress warmly and stay away from slippery shorelines and roads. Aurora activity can never be guaranteed.

Midnight sun & light summer nights

Early summer brings exceptionally bright nights. Tranøy, the lighthouse area and west-facing coastal viewpoints are beautiful places to experience the evening light. Bring an extra layer even in summer.

Arctic Salmon Center · Skutvik

A visitor centre about modern aquaculture and the role of salmon farming in coastal communities. Availability and tours vary by season.

Cycling & winter activities

The quieter roads around Hamarøy are good for scenic cycling, but can be narrow and occasionally gravelly. In winter, conditions may allow snowshoeing, cross-country skiing, winter photography and northern-light viewing. Avoid exposed terrain in storms, poor visibility or avalanche conditions.

Groceries

Tranøy Landhandel is the local grocery option in Tranøy. SPAR Hamarøy at Oppeid is a larger supermarket. Opening hours and self-service access can change, so check current information before relying on late access.

Safety in nature

Weather in Northern Norway can change quickly. Check the forecast, carry a charged phone, wear suitable footwear and clothing, bring water and an extra warm layer, and turn back before conditions become unsafe. Do not rely exclusively on mobile coverage.
