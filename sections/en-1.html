04 · Important

Drinking water

The cabin is supplied by a private mountain water source and is not yet connected to the municipal drinking-water system. Do not drink water directly from the taps.

Your drinking-water options

①Waterdrop A2GFiltered water produced in the kitchen.
②Bottled waterRecommended if you want the highest possible level of certainty.
③Tranøy quayMunicipal drinking water can be collected in your own containers.

Waterdrop A2G reverse-osmosis purifier

The A2G uses multi-stage filtration and reverse osmosis to remove or substantially reduce a wide range of particles and dissolved substances. Because the source is untreated mountain water, we cannot guarantee the microbiological quality of the source water or guarantee that treated water is completely free from microorganisms.

If you want the highest possible level of certainty, use bottled drinking water.

How to use the A2G

  1. Remove the large feed-water tank at the back.
  2. Empty any remaining water.
  3. Fill with fresh, cold tap water to the MAX line.
  4. Place the tank firmly back into the machine.
  5. Allow the machine to filter the water.
  6. Place a glass or bottle under the dispenser.
  7. Select the desired temperature and amount, then dispense.

Important when refilling

Always empty the remaining feed water before refilling. Do not simply top up old water. Never fill above MAX.

The A2G can dispense very hot water. Take care and supervise children around the hot-water function.

Tap water

Tap water can be used for showering, washing and normal household use. For drinking, coffee, tea and cooking, use the A2G, bottled water or municipal drinking water.

Municipal drinking water

There is a drinking-water tap by the quay in Tranøy where you can fill your own containers. Brennvika is expected to be connected to the municipal supply during 2027.

05 · Water safety

Leak protection

Automatic water sensors are installed under the washing machine and dishwasher. If a sensor gets wet, the system may shut off the water and start beeping.

If the water suddenly stops and the alarm beeps

  1. Check underneath the washing machine and dishwasher.
  2. Find the wet sensor and dry the sensor and surrounding floor completely.
  3. Do not reset while the sensor or floor is still wet.
  4. Find the Aqualarm control panel in the cupboard next to the washing machine.
  5. Briefly press OFF.
  6. Then briefly press RESET/ON. The valve should reopen.

Do not keep resetting if there may be a real leak. If the alarm continues or the water does not return, contact us.

Aqualarm instructions ↗

If it is a genuine leak

Stop using water and contact us. Do not dismantle the system or force the valve.

Where are the sensors?

The relevant leak sensors are under the washing machine and dishwasher. The bathroom has a floor drain.

06 · Comfort

Heating & lighting

The cabin automatically adjusts heating for daytime and night-time comfort. You can override the temperature whenever you want.

Heat pump

Use the heat-pump remote control in the living room/kitchen if you want to change the temperature.

Keep exterior doors and windows closed while the heat pump is running.

Underfloor heating

Underfloor heating is installed throughout the cabin. Each room has a wall-mounted thermostat that you may adjust.

Floor heating responds slowly, so allow time for adjustments to take effect.

At checkout

Please leave the heat pump and floor heating switched on. The cabin adjusts the heating automatically after departure.
