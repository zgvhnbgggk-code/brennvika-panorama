07 · Inside the cabin

Kitchen & laundry

A few systems may be unfamiliar to international guests. These are the ones worth knowing.

Induction hob & stove guard

Norwegian kitchens normally use a safety device called a komfyrvakt. It monitors the cooking area and may warn or cut power if it detects unusually high heat.

If it starts beeping while you are cooking

  1. Stay by the hob and reduce the heat.
  2. Check that nothing is burning, overheating or boiling dry.
  3. If everything is under control, press the button on the stove-guard sensor once to acknowledge the warning.

If power to the hob has been switched off

  1. Turn all hob controls completely off.
  2. Check that there is no unsafe overheating.
  3. Allow hot cookware and the cooking area to cool slightly if needed.
  4. Press the button on the stove-guard sensor once to reset.
  5. Switch the hob back on and continue cooking.

If the alarm continues, power does not return or you suspect a real problem, stop using the hob and contact us. Do not remove, cover, disconnect or modify the sensor.

Dishwasher · water supply

The dishwasher has a separate water supply controlled from the kitchen tap/faucet. Please leave this supply open.

If the dishwasher cannot fill: check that the water supply on the tap has not been switched off, open it if needed, then restart the dishwasher. Contact us if the error remains.

Washing machine

The machine is compact and should not be overfilled. Use a small amount of detergent; too much detergent or too much laundry can cause excessive foam.

Before settling in

Please take a quick look around the cabin. If anything is damaged, missing or not working, let us know early. Please do not attempt electrical, plumbing or technical repairs yourself.

Waste

Ordinary household waste goes in the large yellow waste bags used locally. A bag is provided under the kitchen sink. Tie it securely and take it to the roadside waste cage when full or at departure. Use the first cage by the junction towards Vassbotnen.

Do not leave full waste bags outside the cabin; they can attract birds and animals.

08 · Safety

Fire & emergencies

One of the cabin's smoke alarms is connected directly to a professional alarm monitoring service, as required by the insurance company.

If the fire alarm sounds or you discover a fire

  1. If you see or suspect fire, leave the cabin immediately and make sure everyone in your group is accounted for.
  2. Call 110 from a safe location and give the address: Brennvikveien 37, 8294 Hamarøy.
  3. Because the monitored alarm is connected directly to the alarm service, please also call us promptly once everyone is safe — especially if the alarm appears to have been triggered by cooking, steam or another non-fire cause.

09 · Departure

Checkout by 11:00

Please check your booking details for any individually agreed arrangements. Unless otherwise agreed, checkout is by 11:00.

Before leaving

  • Place used dishes in the dishwasher and start the programme.
  • Remove all food and personal belongings from the refrigerator.
  • Leave used towels in the bathroom.
  • Leave the beds as they are.
  • Return furniture and equipment to their original positions.
  • Close all windows and exterior doors.
  • Switch off unnecessary lights and electrical appliances.
  • Do not switch off underfloor heating or the heat pump.
  • Check that you have all personal belongings.
  • Report any damage or technical problems before departure.

Waste

Take the yellow waste bag with you and place it in the designated roadside cage. Use the first waste cage by the junction leading to Vassbotnen.

Lock up

  1. Close the entrance door firmly.
  2. Press the star button *.
  3. Check that the door is securely locked.
