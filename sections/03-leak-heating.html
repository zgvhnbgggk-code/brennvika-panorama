05 · Water safety

Leak protection

Automatic sensors are located under the washing machine and dishwasher. If a sensor gets wet — even accidentally — the system may shut off the water and start beeping.

If the water suddenly stops and you hear beeping

  1. Check underneath the washing machine and dishwasher.
  2. Locate the wet sensor and dry the sensor and surrounding floor completely.
  3. Do not reset while the sensor or floor is still wet.
  4. Locate the beeping Aqualarm control panel in the cupboard next to the washing machine.
  5. Briefly press OFF.
  6. Then briefly press RESET/ON. The valve should reopen and the green light should return.

Do not repeatedly reset the system if there may be a real leak. If the alarm continues or water does not return, contact us.

Open Altech Aqualarm manual / FDV ↗

Why OFF first?

According to the Aqualarm Trådløs+ instructions, a brief press on OFF stops the alarm. A brief press on RESET/ON then resets the system and opens the actuator valve.

If it is a genuine leak

Stop using water, keep the affected area dry if safe to do so, and contact us. Do not dismantle the system or force the valve.

06 · Comfort

Heating & lighting

The cabin is automatically temperature-controlled for daytime and night-time comfort. You can override the settings at any time if you prefer it warmer or cooler.

Automatic climate control

The cabin is heated before your arrival and adjusts the heating automatically through the day and night. You do not need to change anything unless you want a different temperature.

Heat pump

The heat pump serves the living room and kitchen. Use the heat-pump remote control if you want to change the temperature or operating mode.

Keep windows and exterior doors closed while heating or cooling is running.

Underfloor heating

Underfloor heating is available throughout the cabin. Each room has its own wall-mounted thermostat, which you may adjust as needed.

Floor heating responds slowly, so allow some time for a change to take effect.

When you leave

Please leave the heating systems switched on at checkout. The cabin automatically changes to its empty-cabin setting after departure.
