03 · Connection

Wi‑Fi & Starlink

Wi‑Fi is available throughout the cabin. The internet connection is provided through Starlink and is normally fast and reliable.

Network name, password & QR code

You will find the Wi‑Fi details clearly displayed inside the cabin. The sign includes the network name, password and a QR code that can be scanned directly with your phone camera.

Wi‑Fi sign in the cabinScan the QR code on the physical sign

If the connection drops

  1. Check that Wi‑Fi is enabled on your device.
  2. Disconnect and reconnect to the network.
  3. Check the password on the Wi‑Fi sign.
  4. Restart your own phone, tablet or computer.
  5. Wait a few minutes and try again.

Please leave the network equipment alone

Temporary interruptions can occur due to weather, satellite coverage or maintenance. Please do not unplug, move, restart or reset the Starlink equipment or router. Contact us if the connection does not return.

04 · Important

Drinking water

The cabin is supplied by a private mountain water source and is not yet connected to the municipal drinking-water system. Do not drink water directly from the taps.

Waterdrop A2G reverse-osmosis purifier

The A2G uses multi-stage filtration and reverse osmosis to remove or substantially reduce a wide range of particles, dissolved substances and contaminants. Because the source is untreated mountain water, however, we cannot guarantee the microbiological quality of the source water or guarantee that treated water is completely free from microorganisms.

If you want the highest possible level of certainty, use bottled drinking water.

How to use the A2G

  1. Remove the large feed-water tank from the back.
  2. Empty any remaining water.
  3. Fill with fresh, cold tap water to the MAX line.
  4. Place the tank firmly back into the machine.
  5. Allow the machine to filter the water.
  6. Place your glass or bottle under the dispenser.
  7. Select the desired temperature and amount.
  8. Press the dispensing button.

Important when refilling

Always empty the remaining feed water before refilling. Do not simply top up old water. During reverse osmosis, substances removed from the purified water become more concentrated in the water left in the feed tank.

Never fill above the MAX line. The machine can dispense very hot water — supervise children around the hot-water function.

Tap water

Tap water can be used for showering, washing and normal household use. For drinking, coffee, tea and cooking, use the A2G or purchased/municipal drinking water.

Tranøy

There is a municipal drinking-water tap by the quay in Tranøy where you can fill your own containers.
