Salt air. A little patience. A story to tell.

A day on the water.
A memory for years.

For some, the perfect day means watching the sea. For others, it means a fishing rod in hand and the anticipation of the next bite. These photographs are from our own fishing trips — real moments, just as they happened.

Before you go fishing ↓

Make it your own

A quiet moment fishing from a suitable spot on shore, or a trip out by boat. Arrange any boat, equipment or guided trip in advance.

Let the weather decide

Check wind, waves and the marine forecast. Wear a life jacket and warm layers; turn back while conditions are still safe.

Enjoy the catch responsibly

Keep only what you can use. Check current minimum sizes, protected species, closed seasons and rules for taking fish out of Norway.

Before you go fishing

Use permitted tackle and respect private quays, working boats and local restrictions. Keep more than 100 metres from fish farms while fishing. Freshwater fishing has separate rules and may require a permit. Check the official rules for your trip, including any export requirements; a cabin booking is not proof of entitlement to export fish.

Bare feet. Clear water.

Cycling & winter activities

The quieter roads around Hamarøy are good for scenic cycling, but can be narrow and occasionally gravelly. In winter, conditions may allow snowshoeing, cross-country skiing, winter photography and northern-light viewing. Avoid exposed terrain in storms, poor visibility or avalanche conditions.

Safety in nature

Weather in Northern Norway can change quickly. Check the forecast, carry a charged phone, wear suitable footwear and clothing, bring water and an extra warm layer, and turn back before conditions become unsafe. Do not rely exclusively on mobile coverage.

Drinking water

The cabin is supplied by a private mountain water source and is not yet connected to the municipal drinking-water system. Do not drink water directly from the taps.

Your drinking-water options

①Waterdrop A2GFiltered water produced in the kitchen.
②Bottled waterRecommended if you want the highest possible level of certainty.
③Tranøy quayMunicipal drinking water can be collected in your own containers.

Waterdrop A2G reverse-osmosis purifier

The A2G uses multi-stage filtration and reverse osmosis to remove or substantially reduce a wide range of particles and dissolved substances. Because the source is untreated mountain water, we cannot guarantee the microbiological quality of the source water or guarantee that treated water is completely free from microorganisms.

If you want the highest possible level of certainty, use bottled drinking water.

How to use the A2G

  1. Remove the large feed-water tank at the back.
  2. Empty any remaining water.
  3. Fill with fresh, cold tap water to the MAX line.
  4. Place the tank firmly back into the machine.
  5. Allow the machine to filter the water.
  6. Place a glass or bottle under the dispenser.
  7. Select the desired temperature and amount, then dispense.

Important when refilling

Always empty the remaining feed water before refilling. Do not simply top up old water. Never fill above MAX.

The A2G can dispense very hot water. Take care and supervise children around the hot-water function.

Tap water

Tap water can be used for showering, washing and normal household use. For drinking, coffee, tea and cooking, use the A2G, bottled water or municipal drinking water.

Municipal drinking water

There is a drinking-water tap by the quay in Tranøy where you can fill your own containers. Brennvik Panorama is expected to be connected to the municipal supply during 2027.

Kitchen, laundry & waste

A few practical systems are worth knowing, especially if they are unfamiliar to you.

Induction hob & stove guard

Norwegian kitchens normally use a safety device called a komfyrvakt. It monitors the cooking area and may warn or cut power if it detects unusually high heat.

If it starts beeping while you cook

  1. Stay by the hob and reduce the heat.
  2. Check that nothing is burning, overheating or boiling dry.
  3. If everything is under control, press the button on the stove-guard sensor once to acknowledge the warning.

If power to the hob has been switched off

  1. Turn all hob controls completely off.
  2. Check that there is no unsafe overheating.
  3. Let hot cookware cool slightly if needed.
  4. Press the stove-guard sensor button once to reset.
  5. Switch the hob back on.

If the alarm continues or power does not return, stop using the hob and contact us. Do not remove, cover or disconnect the sensor.

Dishwasher · water supply

The dishwasher has a separate water supply controlled from the kitchen tap. Please leave it open.

If the dishwasher cannot fill with water, check the dishwasher water supply at the tap, open it if needed and restart the dishwasher. Contact us if the problem remains.

Washing machine

The washing machine is compact. Do not overfill it, and use only a small amount of detergent. Too much laundry or detergent can cause excessive foam.

Waste

Ordinary household waste goes in the large yellow waste bags used locally. A bag is provided under the kitchen sink. Tie it securely and take it to the roadside waste cage when full or at departure. Use the first cage by the junction towards Vassbotnen.

Do not leave full waste bags outside the cabin; they can attract birds and animals.

Leak protection

Automatic water sensors are installed under the washing machine and dishwasher. If a sensor gets wet, the system may shut off the water and start beeping.

If the water suddenly stops and the alarm beeps

  1. Check underneath the washing machine and dishwasher.
  2. Find the wet sensor and dry the sensor and surrounding floor completely.
  3. Do not reset while the sensor or floor is still wet.
  4. Find the Aqualarm control panel in the cupboard next to the washing machine.
  5. Briefly press OFF.
  6. Then briefly press RESET/ON. The valve should reopen.

Do not keep resetting if there may be a real leak. If the alarm continues or the water does not return, contact us.

Fire & emergencies

One smoke alarm is connected directly to a professional alarm monitoring service, as required by the insurance company.

If the fire alarm sounds or you discover a fire

  1. If you see or suspect fire, leave the cabin immediately and make sure everyone is accounted for.
  2. Call 110 from a safe location and give the address: Brennvikveien 37, 8294 Hamarøy.
  3. Once everyone is safe, please also call us promptly. This is particularly important if the alarm appears to have been triggered by cooking, steam or another non-fire cause.
